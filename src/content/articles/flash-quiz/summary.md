## Summary

Flash Quiz is my bachelor’s thesis project at the University of Trento: a gamified web app that supports university teaching, born from a complete redesign of PolyGlot, an app for learning programming. As the only UX/UI designer during a 375-hour internship, I followed a user-centered, Double Diamond process. I analysed stakeholders and the domain, benchmarked 22 similar products, surveyed 163 students and 71 teachers, and interviewed 7 students and 2 teachers. Students asked for a pressure-free way to study, explanations of their mistakes and cooperation rather than individual competition; teachers wanted detailed data on how their class is learning. I turned these needs into pain points and personas, then, as project constraints required, went straight to high-fidelity mockups in Material Design 3.

The result is two connected products. Students (on PC and phone) get courses, quizzes and flashcards, study groups and private analytics with explanations. Teachers (on large screens) get course management and class analytics by topic and over time. The gamification balances intrinsic and extrinsic motivation: in courses teams compete, not individuals, and boosters keep the competition balanced (Octalysis score: 169). There was no time for user testing, so I wrote a testing plan: heuristic evaluation, user observation, then SUS/UEQ.
